Zhejiang University

Qian Feng

I’m a PhD student in computer science at Zhejiang University. I study how models learn new tasks, retain what they’ve learned, and forget selected concepts. This work spans continual learning, machine unlearning, and multimodal models.

As a research intern in Ant Group’s Plan A program, I work with the LingDT team on post-training language models. I’m currently working on coding agents and on ways to generate and evolve the environments they learn in.
